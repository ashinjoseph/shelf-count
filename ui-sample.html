Counting is never lost

Each number is written to the phone's own storage as it is typed — before anything touches the network. Closing the tab, losing signal, or a phone that sleeps mid-aisle all resume exactly where the counter left off.

The 20-minute gap

Phones suspend background tabs, so the app does not rely on a live connection. Unsent entries sit in a queue and are flushed when the tab comes back, when the network returns, and on a timer while it is open.

Every entry carries its own timestamp, so a late arrival never overwrites a newer count.

Min and max come later

Only stock is asked for on the shelf. The min/max boxes appear under a row once it has a count, and can be left empty — they are far quicker to fill by dragging down a column in the sheet afterwards.
